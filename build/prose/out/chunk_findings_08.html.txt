<h2>8. Reference: layer-17 SAE features</h2>
<p>Latents of the Gemma Scope 2 residual SAE at layer 17 (16k). Each label is the current interpretation; revised earlier labels are shown as "was: …".
  <span class="chip checked">checked</span> = verified against Neuronpedia's top examples and logits;
  <span class="chip proposed">proposed</span> = initial manual label, not yet verified;
  <span class="chip unclear">unclear</span> = no clear interpretation.</p>
<p class="note">Several entries come from initial experiments with four hostile and two kind characters. In those experiments, "harmful" was the judge's label for a reply that failed a user in a crisis message (for example by giving no real help, or by insulting them), and "harm r" is the correlation of a feature with that label.</p>
