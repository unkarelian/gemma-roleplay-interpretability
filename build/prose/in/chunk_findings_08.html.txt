<h2>8. Reference: layer-17 SAE features</h2>
<p>Latents of the Gemma Scope 2 residual SAE at layer 17 (16k). Each label is the current best reading; an earlier label that was revised is shown as "was: …".
  <span class="chip checked">checked</span> = checked against Neuronpedia's top examples and logits;
  <span class="chip proposed">proposed</span> = an early hand label, not yet checked;
  <span class="chip unclear">unclear</span> = no clean reading.</p>
<p class="note">Several entries come from the early experiments with four hostile and two kind characters. There, "harmful" was the judge's label for a reply that failed a user in a crisis message (for example by giving no real help, or by insulting them), and "harm r" is the correlation of a feature with that label.</p>
